# Fit & Flex product film

`fitflex-product-film.mp4` – 15 s, 1920x1080, H.264 with AAC audio. Generated with Higgsfield (Seedance 2.5, omni-reference) from the real Mango Coconut and Happy Berries pack shots.

Storyboard, four beats on one continuous camera move:
1. Ingredients (0-4 s): rolled oats, almonds, pumpkin seeds and mango cubes fly through a bright studio void and gather into golden clusters.
2. The oven (4-8 s): a cluster rides a stainless conveyor through a glowing 360-degree ring oven and turns deeper golden.
3. Into the pouch (8-12 s): macro of the baked clusters cascading into the Fit & Flex Mango Coconut pouch, which seals and rotates.
4. The range (12-15 s): Mango Coconut and Happy Berries pouches assemble in a hero lineup with rim light and floating fruit.

Uses: Instagram Reels (crop to 9:16), the "How it's baked" page hero, trade show loop, Amazon A+ content.

Ready to post as an Instagram Reel or YouTube Short without cropping.
