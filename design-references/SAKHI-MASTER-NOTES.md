# Reference: sakhimaster.com (and similar Indian D2C food sites)

## What Sakhi Master does (Shopify, custom theme)
Announcement bar > sticky nav with search/account/cart > split hero (bold uppercase Oswald headline with a green accent line, pill eyebrow, pack shot on a blob, floating product thumbnails, two CTAs) > 3-number stats row > 4-icon feature strip > bundles carousel > product grid with size chips and quick-add > best-seller feature block > "Our story" with 4 numbered points and a YouTube embed > 5-step "How it works" > testimonial marquee > newsletter > contact cards > footer with payment methods. Palette: green #2E7D32, orange #FF6400, off-white #F8F9F6. Fonts: Oswald + Inter.
