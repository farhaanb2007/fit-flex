# Fit & Flex redesign — three Refero references

Current site (fitandflex.in): Shopify default theme, no hero, no brand story, generic product grid, emoji nav, a 25%-off banner as the first thing you see. Nothing communicates "baked, oat-based, Shark Tank, 4-acre plant, export brand." That is the gap the redesign has to close.

Screenshots in this folder are Refero thumbnails (desktop). Full galleries need a free Refero login.

---

## 1. Seed (seed.com) — Health & Wellness
Refero: https://refero.design/search?site_id[id][]=923
Files: seed1–4.jpg

**Why it fits:** Seed sells a science-backed daily health product (probiotics) the same way Fit & Flex should sell prebiotic-fibre, slow-baked oats. The sharks attacked Fit & Flex on health claims; Seed's design shows how to make claims feel credible instead of salesy.

**Patterns to lift**
- Hero: one confident sentence ("A life-changing health routine, built for your microbiome") + soft product-in-context photo. Fit & Flex version: "Baked, not fried. Oats, not sugar." with the granola pouches on a breakfast table.
- Two CTAs side by side: primary "Take the quiz" / secondary "Shop now". Fit & Flex: "Find my snack" quiz (breakfast vs snack, protein vs low sugar) + "Shop".
- Mega-menu drawer (seed2–4): Shop lists SKUs with tiny pack thumbnails + "Save 25%" pill; Science tab lists Approach / Labs / Scientists; Learn tab lists articles. Fit & Flex: Shop by range (Granola, Muesli, Power Oats, Protein Puffs, Multigrain, Mini Bites), "How it's made" (360° slow-bake, 4-acre plant, ISO/BRC/Halal), "Learn" (blog).
- Top strip is a soft brand-green notice, not a screaming red discount bar.
- Palette: near-black text, off-white, one deep green, one acid-green accent. Map to Fit & Flex earthy brown + orange from the Therefore Design identity.

## 2. Douze (drinkdouze.com) — Food & Drink
Refero: https://refero.design/search?site_id[id][]=688
Files: douze1–5.jpg

**Why it fits:** A small D2C consumables brand with several flavours, built on Shopify like Fit & Flex. Shows how to make packaged food look premium and flavour-led without a big budget.

**Patterns to lift**
- Flavour = colour system (douze4): each product gets its own gradient background and one editorial word. Fit & Flex: Mango Coconut = mango-orange gradient, Happy Berries = berry-pink, Choco Almond = cocoa-brown, Mixed Fruit = green. Use for hero carousel, collection cards and PDP backgrounds.
- Product page (douze5): 50/50 split, sticky right column with eyebrow ("Social bubbly"), display-serif name, short sensory description, quantity chips (1 / 6), primary + secondary buy buttons, thumbnail strip below image. Fit & Flex: pack-size chips (275g / 450g / 22g sachet x14), "Add to cart" + "Subscribe & save".
- Big serif + small caps sans type pairing; editorial headline split across the hero ("Conviviality … compromise-free"). Fit & Flex: "Crave it. … Keep it clean."
- Cut-out newsletter card floating over the hero with a -10% first-order hook (replaces the current 25% top bar).
- Utility bar: free shipping threshold + language/currency switch. Fit & Flex ships to UAE/GCC, so an INR/AED switch is a real, differentiating feature.

## 3. On (on.com) — Fitness / Lifestyle
Refero: https://refero.design/search?site_id[id][]=809
Files: on1–3.jpg

**Why it fits:** The founder is a marathoner and the brand is literally called "Fit". On shows how performance brands use full-bleed human photography and short declarative copy to sell an active lifestyle, not just a product. This is the emotional layer the current site has none of.

**Patterns to lift**
- Full-bleed lifestyle hero with copy bottom-left, two pill buttons (on1). Fit & Flex: runner / gym-bag / office-desk shot with "Fuel that's baked, not fried." + "Shop granola" / "See how it's made".
- Minimal nav: three words (Shop / Activities / Explore) + icons. Fit & Flex: Shop / Why baked / Explore. Drop the emoji nav.
- Community / membership section (on2) — reuse for "Fit & Flex Club": Shark Tank story, marathon sponsorships, Garba nights, UGC from Instagram.
- Referral module (on3): "Share with a friend and both save 10%" with a two-column form + energetic group-run photo. Cheap to build on Shopify, and it gives the owner a growth mechanic.

## How the three combine into one site
| Section | Borrow from |
|---|---|
| Announcement bar, nav, mega-menu | Seed |
| Homepage hero | On (photo + copy) with Douze flavour colour behind product shots |
| "Find my snack" quiz | Seed |
| Product range cards | Douze colour-per-flavour |
| Product page | Douze split layout + Seed's science/claims block |
| "How it's made" (plant, slow-bake, certifications) | Seed Science tab |
| Community / Shark Tank / athletes | On |
| Referral + newsletter | On + Douze |
| Footer with INR/AED switch | Douze |

Type: one display serif (e.g. Fraunces / Instrument Serif) + one grotesk (e.g. Inter / General Sans).
Colour: keep the brand brown + orange, add off-white and one flavour accent per page.
