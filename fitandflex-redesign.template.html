Eat healthy, live well.

Fit & Flex started in December 2019 when Pathik Patel, a marathon runner from Ahmedabad, went looking for a breakfast that was neither bland health food nor a sugar bomb in disguise. He could not find one, so he built a plant to make it.

Today Niva Nutrifoods bakes every Fit & Flex pack on European ovens in a four-acre facility, ships to over 6,000 stores in India and to 30 countries, and still tastes every batch before it leaves.

We want Fit & Flex to be India's most loved snacking brand in the better-for-you category.
Pathik Patel, Founder

From oat to pack

Five steps, one building. Nothing leaves Ahmedabad until it has been through all of them.

  1. 1

    Source

    Whole rolled oats, seeds and nuts chosen for size and moisture, then cleaned.

  2. 2

    Mix

    Oats bound with honey or jaggery in small batches so every cluster holds together.

  3. 3

    Slow bake

    Baked on a 360-degree conveyor oven. Low heat, long time, deep crunch.

  4. 4

    Add fruit

    Freeze-dried fruit and chocolate are folded in after baking so they stay intact.

  5. 5

    Seal and ship

    Nitrogen-flushed, zip-lock pouches packed for retail, online and export.
