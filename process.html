From oat to pack

Five steps, one building. Click a step to jump to it, or just keep scrolling.

Use arrow keys to move between steps.

1

Source

Rolled oats are graded for flake size and moisture before they enter the plant. Seeds, nuts and freeze-dried fruit come from audited suppliers and are held in a separate cold store, away from the bakery floor.

What we check
  • Oat flake thickness and moisture on every lot
  • Supplier certificates for nuts and seeds
  • Fruit is freeze-dried, never candied or sulphur-treated
  • Allergen segregation: nuts stored on their own line
Why it matters

A thin, dry oat flake bakes into a crisp cluster. A soft one turns to dust. Grading at the door is the cheapest quality control in the building.

100%rolled oats, never instant0artificial coloursSeparatecold store for fruit
2

Mix

Oats, wheat flakes and rice crisps are coated with honey or jaggery syrup and a small amount of sunflower oil. This is where the cluster forms. Batches are kept small so every pouch has the same ratio of oat to nut to fruit.

What we check
  • Syrup temperature and coating weight per batch
  • Oligofructose dosed here for prebiotic fibre
  • No palm oil anywhere in the plant
  • Recipe locked by batch card, signed by the line lead
Why it matters

Most granola gets its crunch from sugar and fat. Ours gets it from the bake, which is why the added sugar stays under a teaspoon per serve.

<1 tspadded sugar per serveSunfloweroil, not palmGI 0prebiotic fibre (oligofructose)
3

Slow bake

The mix travels through a European 360-degree conveyor oven at low heat for a long time. Frying would take ninety seconds and add fat. Baking takes far longer and adds nothing. The result is a cluster that stays crunchy in milk.

What we check
  • Oven zone temperatures logged continuously
  • Colour and moisture of clusters at the exit
  • Metal detection on every belt
  • Crunch test: a sample sits in milk for ten minutes
Why it matters

This oven is the whole bet. It cost around fifteen crore, it is the only line of its kind in India, and it is why "baked, not fried" is a fact rather than a slogan.

375 MTmonthly capacity4 acresunder one roofOnly oneof its kind in India
4

Add fruit

Freeze-dried mango, berries, apple and pineapple are folded in only after the bake has cooled, so the fruit keeps its colour, shape and taste instead of turning brown in the oven. Chocolate and cookie pieces go in the same way.

What we check
  • Cluster temperature before fruit is added
  • Fruit-to-oat ratio by weight, not by eye
  • Visual check for colour on every tray
Why it matters

Freeze-drying removes water and keeps everything else. Candied fruit is mostly sugar. The difference is visible in the bowl and on the label.

Freeze-driednot candied2 applesworth of fibre per serveRealcocoa and almonds
5

Seal and ship

Pouches are nitrogen-flushed to keep oxygen out and sealed with a zip lock so the crunch survives the first week after opening. Cartons leave for six thousand Indian stores, Amazon and Flipkart, and container loads for the Gulf.

What we check
  • Seal integrity and residual oxygen per lot
  • Batch code and best-before printed on every pouch
  • Retained samples kept for the full shelf life
  • Export lots audited to BRC and Halal
Why it matters

A batch code on the pack means any pouch, anywhere, can be traced back to the day, the line and the oats it came from.
