Questions we get a lot

How long does delivery take?

Two to five working days across India. Metro cities usually see it in two. UAE orders ship through our distributor and noon Minutes.

Is delivery free?

Yes on orders above ₹499. Below that, standard courier charges apply at checkout.

Can I return a pack?

Unopened packs can be returned within seven days of delivery for a full refund. Opened packs with a quality issue: send us a photo and the batch code and we will replace it.

Is the granola vegan?

The granolas use honey, so they are vegetarian but not vegan. The Zero Added Sugar Muesli and the Protein Puffs are vegan.

Where is it made?

Everything is baked and packed at our own plant near Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Nothing is outsourced.

Do you do private label?

Yes. Choose "Interested in private label" in the form above and tell us the category and volume you have in mind.
