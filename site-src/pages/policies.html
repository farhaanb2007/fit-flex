Delivery

Orders are packed at our plant near Ahmedabad and handed to the courier within one working day. Delivery across India takes two to five working days; metro cities usually see it in two.

  • Free delivery on orders above ₹499. Below that, the courier charge is shown at checkout before you pay.
  • You get a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as the parcel is scanned.
  • UAE orders are fulfilled by our distributor and through noon Minutes in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.
  • Cash on delivery is available across India for orders under ₹3,000.

Delivery threshold and courier partners are placeholders for the live store settings.

Returns and refunds

Unopened packs can be returned within seven days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Refunds are issued within five working days of the parcel reaching us.

  • Opened pack with a quality issue: email a photo and the batch code printed on the back of the pouch and we will replace it, no return needed.
  • Damaged in transit: tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we ship a replacement.
  • Combo packs are returned as a whole, not as individual pouches.

Privacy policy

Niva Nutrifoods LLP collects the details needed to take and deliver your order: name, address, phone, email and payment confirmation. Payments are processed by our payment gateway; we never see or store your card number.

  • We email you about your order, and about new products only if you subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Your saved bag lives in your own browser until you sign in.
  • We do not sell personal data. Couriers receive only what they need to deliver the parcel.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, write to us through the contact form.
