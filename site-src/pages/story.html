The founder

Pathik Patel is an engineer by training and, in his own words, a non-elite runner and a gym junkie. A high blood pressure reading at 35 sent him out on the road, and a supermarket shelf with nothing honest on it sent him into the kitchen. In December 2019 he founded Fit & Flex to bake the breakfast he could not buy: oat-rich, slow-baked, real fruit, no palm oil.

Rather than outsource, he built a four-acre plant outside Ahmedabad with his own money and the only 360-degree baking line of its kind in India. In 2024 he took the brand to Shark Tank India, argued on air about what "healthy" should mean, and came home to eleven times the orders. He still runs, from marathons to the Great Wall of China to HYROX in Abu Dhabi and Delhi, and he still reads the back of every pack before the front. His motto is three sentences long: be big, be fast, be bold.

Seven years, one oven

December 2019Fit & Flex launches in Gujarat

Four crunchy granolas in resealable pouches. First listings in Ahmedabad grocery stores and on Amazon.

2020The plant

A four-acre facility outside Ahmedabad with European baking lines and 375 metric tons of monthly capacity. Still the only plant of its kind in India.

2021Muesli joins the range

High protein and zero added sugar muesli, 83% whole grains, no artificial ingredients.

2022Multigrain mixture and Mini Bites

Baked alternatives to fried namkeen, plus chocolate-coated granola bites.

2023Exports begin

UAE first, then Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, the Philippines and Nigeria. Star Export House recognition from the Government of India.

March 2024Shark Tank India, Season 3

No deal, a national conversation about sugar labels, and an eleven-fold sales spike the next morning.

2025Power Oats, Protein Puffs, and the Tata Mumbai Marathon

Ready-to-eat high protein oats and roasted millet puffs launch. The brand shows up at the Tata Mumbai Marathon 2025. Six thousand retail stores across India.

Certified, not just claimed

Every certification below is audited at the plant, not applied for on paper.

ISO 22000:2018

Food safety management system covering sourcing, baking, packing and storage.

BRC Global Standard

The retail food safety standard required by supermarket chains in the UK, Europe and the Gulf.

Halal certified

Every product and every line, audited annually for GCC distribution.

FSSAI licensed

India's food regulator. Licence number printed on every pack.

APEDA registered

Registered exporter with the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority.

Star Export House

Government of India recognition for consistent export performance.
