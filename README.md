# Fit & Flex website redesign

Multi-page website concept for Fit & Flex (Niva Nutrifoods LLP, Ahmedabad): oat-based granola, muesli, Power Oats and Protein Puffs.

- The site lives at the repo root: `index.html`, `shop.html`, `product.html`, `story.html`, `process.html`, `contact.html`, `policies.html` and `assets/`. Open `index.html` or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server 8765`). Vercel deploys it as a static site with no build step.
- `site-src/` – editable source: `partials/` (header, footer), `pages/`, and `build.py` which stitches them into the root pages.
- `design-references/` – Refero picks, Sakhi Master notes and product images used during design.
- `fit-and-flex-brand-research.md` – brand, founder and market research behind the copy.
- `fitandflex-redesign.html` – the earlier single-page concept, kept for reference.

Hero and process videos and the lifestyle stills were generated with Higgsfield from the brand's real pack shots. Product images and logo are the brand's own. See `SITE-NOTES.md` for the list of placeholders to replace before launch.
