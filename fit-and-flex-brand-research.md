## 2. Founder & team
- **Pathik Patel** — Designated Partner, Niva Nutrifoods LLP; Director of the new Pvt Ltd. Family runs a ₹700-800 Cr cotton ginning / chemical trading business. Started Fit & Flex after a high-blood-pressure health scare at 35. Also runs other businesses (a point the sharks criticised).
- **Archana Pathik Patel** — co-director of Niva Nutrifood Products Pvt Ltd.
- Partners listed on the About page: **Ravi Patel** (electrical engineer, real estate), **Dhairya Lakhani** (chemical engineer, ops/technical), **Prashant Prajapati** (business veteran, real estate).
- A "Dixit Patel" lists himself on LinkedIn as Managing Director at Niva Nutri Foods (unverified).

## 4. Manufacturing
- 4-acre plant near Ahmedabad (exact site not published), described as "the only one of its kind in India" and "among Asia's largest cereal plants".
- ~₹15 Cr European machinery; capacity 375 MT/month.
- Plant reportedly ~70% under-utilised at time of pitch → private-label / contract manufacturing is a stated growth lever (exhibits at Private Label Middle East). Private label contributed ~15% extra revenue.

## 6. Distribution
- **India offline (~70% of revenue):** 5,000-6,000+ GT/MT stores incl. Reliance Fresh/Signature, SPAR; 200+ vending machines. 2024 goal: +10,000 GT stores. Early ambition (2020): 60,000 stores in Gujarat alone.
- **India online:** Amazon.in brand store (seller NIVA NUTRIFOODS LLP), Flipkart, BigBasket, own Shopify D2C (running 25%-off sitewide promo).
- **International:** 30+ countries claimed; core markets UAE (500→750 stores via Finefair / Al Seer Group, noon Minutes), Saudi, Kuwait, Oman, Philippines, Nigeria, Maldives, Africa. Export records (Feb-Apr 2026): 297 shipments, 49% to Finefair Commercial Complex LLC (UAE). HS 19041090 / 21069099.
- Trade shows: Gulfood 2025 & 2026 (Dubai), Saudi Food Show 2025, Private Label Middle East 2026; booked for Gulfood 2027 (North Hall 1, N1-J24).

## 7. Brand identity
- Built end-to-end by **Therefore Design** (Pune/Mumbai) in 2019-20: name, positioning, packaging, website.
- Target: fitness-conscious 28-45 urban consumers. Visual concept "Forest of Goodness" — paper-cut forest layers, earthy brown + vibrant orange, narrow "lean" logotype. Resealable zipper pouches instead of boxes.
- Launch event featured Mandira Bedi, Irfan Pathan, Parthiv Patel. Marketing via marathons, Garba nights, fitness influencers.
- Current Instagram positioning leans harder on protein ("filled with protein", Protein Puffs launch Dec 2024) and "mindful snacking".

## 8. Shark Tank India (S3, Ep 40, aired 15 Mar 2024)
- Ask: ₹1 Cr for 3% (₹33.33 Cr valuation). Result: **no deal**.
- Aman Gupta & Vineeta Singh out: founder split across 3 businesses. Deepinder Goyal & Anupam Mittal out: "misleading" health claims (oligofructose / "Eat healthy" tagline). Anupam: "Don't lie about health."
- Namita Thapar: ₹25 L for 2.5% + ₹75 L debt @10% for 3 yrs, conditional on machinery moving into a separate entity (implied ~₹10 Cr valuation). Declined.
- Immediate effect: founder reports 11x sales spike next day, 8x the following week.

## 9. Controversies & reputation
- **Founder vs sharks (Aug 2024, Raj Shamani podcast):** Patel called the treatment "very unreasonable, very unfair", argued oligofructose has GI 0 vs sugar 68, alleged unfavourable editing. Reddit r/sharktankindia sentiment was mixed but many sided with him against Deepinder.
- **Legal notice to Sony (26 Jun 2024):** Sony flagged the brand's episode clips as trademark violations ~25 days after airing (guidelines allegedly allowed 3 months). Facebook page unpublished, ad account blocked, collab features disabled → founder claims 50 days of zero D2C sales and a 10-12x downturn vs a projected 7x lift. Sought account reinstatement; said 70+ startups faced similar issues. No public Sony response found.
- Note the new Pvt Ltd was incorporated 27 Dec 2023, shortly before the episode aired, possibly the "separate entity" structure the sharks pushed for.

## 10. Competitive set
Yoga Bar (ITC-acquired), Open Secret, Meal of the Moment, True Elements, Kellogg's/Quaker/Bagrry's (mass), The Whole Truth, Slurrp Farm. Tracxn ranks Fit & Flex 40th of 148 tracked competitors; score 33/100.

## 11. Assessment (strengths / risks)
**Strengths:** owns its plant and rare baking tech; certifications for export; genuine offline reach for a bootstrapped brand; strong GCC distribution; clear design identity; founder has deep pockets.
**Risks:** thin margins (5% EBITDA) with heavy distributor take; founder attention split; small team (~16); low marketing spend; public spat with a national TV franchise; claims-marketing scrutiny; capacity under-utilisation; no institutional capital while rivals are funded.
